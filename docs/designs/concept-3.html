Only the people you choose can read it

The plan is to lock your record on your own device before it is sent. The server stores it but cannot read it. This uses the sgit design and AES-256-GCM encryption.

This is a mock-up. Nothing here is really encrypted, and how keys are recovered is still being worked out.
