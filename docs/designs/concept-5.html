About the registry

What happens to a knee in the ten years after ligament surgery?

This registry sets out to answer that, one operation at a time, with scores from the patients themselves.

Read a sample record

Patients

Your scans, letters, operation notes and scores sit in one record. We write to you when a score is due. You decide who may read it.

Clinicians

A knee's full history on one page. Op notes, imaging and scores side by side. Your own outcomes set against the national figures, and a short form to close each case.

Administrators

A back office for clinic teams. Register a patient, take a short triage history and keep track of consent, missing data and overdue follow-up.

How records are protected

The design follows the sgit vault model. Each record is encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM before upload. The server holds the data but never sees its contents, file names or keys.

Note. This mock-up contains no real encryption. Losing a vault key means losing the data, so key recovery must be settled before any real record is stored.
