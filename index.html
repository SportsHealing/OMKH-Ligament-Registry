Proposed redraft of the UK National Ligament Registry

A clear record of every knee ligament repair.

Surgeons, patients and clinic teams add to one record. Patients score their knee before surgery and for ten years after. The registry tracks which repairs last.

Who the registry is for

For patients

Score your knee in a few minutes and see your results straight away. You choose who can see your record.

Read the patient guide

For clinicians

The whole knee history of your own patients on one screen. A short form at the end of each case.

Read the clinician guide

For clinic teams

Register patients from the keyboard. Worklists show missing consent, overdue scores and unfinished forms.

Read the admin guide

Four short scores

IKDC, KOOS, a short form pain score and SANE. Before surgery, then at 1, 2, 5 and 10 years. What the scores mean.

Keeping data safe

Some of this is planned and not yet built. Each point says which.

  • Encrypted on your device before upload
    Planned. sgit vault design, in beta.
  • You choose who sees your record
    Shown in this demo.
  • Every access is logged
    Shown in this demo.
  • UK GDPR and NHS data security standards
    Planned. Not yet assessed.
  • Invented data in this mock-up
    No real login or encryption here.
