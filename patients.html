Scores

What your scores mean

You fill in four short scores before surgery and at 1, 2, 5 and 10 years.

IKDC. 18 questions about what your knee lets you do. One score from 0 to 100. Higher is better: 90 or more is normal, 80 to 89 near normal, 70 to 79 abnormal and under 70 severely abnormal. About the IKDC score.

KOOS. 42 questions in five parts: pain, symptoms, daily living, sport and quality of life. Each part is scored from 0 to 100. Higher is better. About the KOOS score.

Short form pain score. How bad your knee pain is, from 0 (no pain) to 10 (the worst pain you can imagine). Lower is better. About knee scores.

SANE. One question: how would you rate your knee today, as a percentage of normal? 0 to 100%. Higher is better. About the SANE score.

Your scores help surgeons learn which operations last. They are not used to judge you, and this site does not give medical advice. Talk to your care team about your own knee.
